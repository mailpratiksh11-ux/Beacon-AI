Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Beacon AI is an email outreach tool. You connect a mailbox, upload a list of contacts, and Beacon AI sends personalized emails on your behalf. This policy explains exactly what data that involves, where it goes, and how long we keep it.

1. Who we are

Beacon AI ("we", "us") operates the service at beacon.heypratikk.com. For any privacy question, contact mailpratiksh11@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Account information

  • Your name and email address
  • A hashed password, if you signed up with email and password. We never store your password in readable form.
  • If you signed in with Google or Microsoft: your email address and display name from that account

Mailbox connection

  • SMTP users: your mail server address, port, username and password
  • Google and Microsoft users: the access and refresh tokens that let us send on your behalf

Both are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being written to the database. See section 5.

Content you create and upload

  • Contacts you import — names, email addresses, company names and any other fields in your file
  • Email templates and campaign content you write
  • A send history: recipient, subject, delivery status and timestamp for each email

Technical data

Standard server logs: IP address, browser type and timestamps of requests. These are used to operate and secure the service.

3. Google user data

If you connect a Google account, Beacon AI requests these scopes and uses them only as described:

ScopeWhat we do with it
userinfo.email
userinfo.profile		Identify your account and show your name and email in the interface.
gmail.sendSend the emails you compose, from your own address. We send only what you have explicitly queued or scheduled.
gmail.readonlyDisplay messages and replies in the in-app Inbox so you can see responses to your campaigns without leaving Beacon AI.

Limited Use disclosure. Beacon AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, and without exception:

  • We do not use Gmail data to train, improve or fine-tune any artificial intelligence or machine learning model, whether ours or a third party's.
  • We do not transfer Gmail message content to our AI provider. The writing assistant receives only text you type into the composer yourself.
  • We do not sell, rent or share Gmail data with data brokers, advertisers or any party for advertising purposes.
  • No human at Beacon AI reads your Gmail messages, except where you have given explicit permission for a specific support issue, where it is necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or where required by law.

Messages read through gmail.readonly are fetched when you open the Inbox and are displayed to you. You can revoke Beacon AI's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. Microsoft account data

If you connect a Microsoft account, we request permission to read your profile and send mail through Microsoft Graph. The same restrictions in section 3 apply: no AI training, no transfer to advertisers, no sale. You can revoke access in your Microsoft account settings.

5. How your data is stored and secured

  • Mail passwords and OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are written to the database. They are decrypted only in memory, at the moment an email is sent.
  • All traffic between your browser and our servers uses TLS.
  • Data is stored in MongoDB Atlas, which encrypts data at rest.
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes and cannot be recovered by us or anyone else.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We will notify affected users without undue delay if we become aware of a breach involving personal data.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account data, contacts and campaign history are kept while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, your data is removed from our systems within 30 days, including stored credentials and OAuth tokens.
  • Server logs are retained for a limited period for security and debugging.

To delete your account and all associated data, email mailpratiksh11@gmail.com. Disconnecting a mailbox in Settings deletes the stored credentials for that mailbox immediately.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact mailpratiksh11@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Contacts you upload

When you upload a contact list, you are responsible for having a lawful basis to hold and email those addresses. We process that data on your behalf and only to deliver the campaigns you create. We do not use your contact lists for our own purposes, and we never add them to any shared or cross-account database.

10. Children

Beacon AI is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will update the date at the top of this page and notify you by email before the change takes effect.
