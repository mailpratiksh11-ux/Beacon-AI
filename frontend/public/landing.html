The current process

Outreach is not hard. It is just repetitive.

Most people run outreach out of a spreadsheet and their inbox. It works, right up until the list gets long enough that the work becomes the bottleneck instead of the writing.

01 Find the contact
02 Write the email
03 Swap the name and company
04 Send it
05 Note it in the sheet
06 Do it 147 more times

Repeat per recipient  ·  148 recipients = 148 rounds of the same six steps

With Beacon

Write it once. Beacon handles the other 147.

You build the list, write one version with merge fields, and set the pace. Beacon personalizes each email, sends them in order, and records what landed and what did not. You keep the writing. It takes the repetition.

How it works

Four steps, then it runs on its own.

STEP 01

Add your contacts

Import a CSV or pick from contacts you have already saved. Beacon reads the columns and maps the email, name and company fields for you. Group them however you want to send to them.

STEP 02

Write one email, personalized

Drop in merge fields and Beacon fills them per recipient. Save it as a template to reuse later, or let the AI assistant draft a first version and rewrite it until it sounds like you.

Set the pace and launch

Choose the gap between emails so you are not firing 148 messages in a minute. Attach a file if every recipient needs one. Send now, or schedule the whole campaign for a better hour.

Capabilities

What Beacon actually does.

No feature list padding. These are the things you can do in the product today.

Delivery and failure, per recipient

Every send is recorded with its outcome. Bad addresses and bounces are marked so you stop mailing them, and you can resend a single failure without rerunning the campaign.

Campaigns you can resume

Stop halfway and come back. Beacon knows who has already been contacted and picks up from there.

Reusable templates

Save the versions that work, with their merge fields intact, and pull them into a new campaign.

Paced, sequential sending

One email at a time with a gap you choose, rather than a burst that looks like a blast.

Your inbox, your identity

Connect Gmail, Outlook or any SMTP account. Mail leaves from your address and replies come back to you.

Contact groups

Tag and filter your list so a campaign goes to the right slice of it, not all of it.

Attachments on every send

A deck, a resume, a one-pager — attached to each personalized email automatically.

Schedule for later

Queue a campaign now and have it start at a time your recipients are more likely to be reading.

Why we built it

Automation should remove the repetitive work, not the control.

Plenty of tools will send thousands of emails for you and hide every decision behind a dashboard. Beacon does the part that is genuinely mechanical — the copying, the pasting, the sending, the record-keeping — and leaves the part that matters with you. You still write it. You still choose who gets it. You can still stop it halfway through.
